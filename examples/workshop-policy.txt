Fictional workshop laser-cutter policy, revision 1. This is a software demonstration, not instructions for operating machinery.

Anyone whose workshop access is revoked must not use the workshop laser cutter, regardless of staff status or supervision. This revocation rule overrides every permission below.

When access is not revoked, staff members may use the laser cutter with or without a supervisor present.

When access is not revoked, non-staff operators may use the laser cutter only while a qualified supervisor is present. Non-staff operators must not use it without a qualified supervisor present.

These three conditions completely determine permission under this fictional policy. No other conditions or exceptions apply.
